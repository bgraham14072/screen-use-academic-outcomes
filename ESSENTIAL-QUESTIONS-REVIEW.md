# Essential Questions: Citation Map and Review Flags

Draft for co-author review. Prepared November 18, 2026, from the same answer data used by the dashboard; no author approvals are recorded. The page remains a draft until all five authors approve the final wording.

This map lists the existing dashboard records, narrative cards and planning tools used by each answer. No new research sources or numerical estimates were added. Research labels describe the scope of the existing collection, not formal quality grades; their definitions appear on the [Methods page](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#answer-confidence).

## Where the evidence is thin

The largest direct-evidence gaps concern a school-day limit (Q6), the cause of national score declines (Q8), the effects of Sweden’s policy (Q9), extending phone rules to instructional devices (Q10), removing technology and equity (Q11), learner-specific access needs (Q12), and the proposed AI implementations (Q13–15). Q16 offers a planning approach, not a validated decision or stopping rule.

Q4 cannot quantify adult support across ages and tools; Q5 mainly draws on college note-taking; Q7 lacks a clean causal comparison of school and home use. These are scope limits, not claims that nothing is known. Q14’s distinction between detecting a difficulty and improving reading is clear, but the particular tools’ learning benefits remain unestablished in this collection.

## LEARNING AND READING

1. **[Does technology help children learn, and does it matter how it's used?](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#q1-learning)**
   - Research label: mixed.
   - Existing evidence: [D1 · Mobile literacy & numeracy lessons](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#record-D1); [D2 · Higher-risk-study sensitivity check](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#record-D2); [L1 · Technology-delivered literacy instruction](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#record-L1); [N4 · Randomized reading-technology studies](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#record-N4).
   - Review flag: Mixed findings, not an evidence gap; no confident claim about every tool.

2. **[Is reading on paper better than reading on a screen? When is print the better choice?](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#q2-paper-reading)**
   - Research label: fairly consistent.
   - Existing evidence: [R1 · Paper versus screen comprehension](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#record-R1); [Delgado et al. · Comparable reading texts](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#paper-delgado); [Clinton · Paper reading and genre limits](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#paper-clinton); [Furenes et al. · Reading features and adult support](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#reading-furenes).
   - Review flag: The scoped comprehension finding is fairly consistent; universal format rules are not supported.

3. **[Which e-book and reading-app features help, and which get in the way?](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#q3-reading-features)**
   - Research label: mixed.
   - Existing evidence: [Furenes et al. · Reading features and adult support](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#reading-furenes); [Takacs et al. · Multimedia and interactive features](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#reading-takacs); [Munzer et al. · Parent–toddler reading interactions](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#reading-munzer).
   - Review flag: Mixed feature findings; existing evidence cannot rank individual current apps.

4. **[How much does a teacher's or parent's involvement matter?](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#q4-adult-support)**
   - Research label: mixed.
   - Existing evidence: [S6 · Parent co-viewing and language](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#record-S6); [Furenes et al. · Reading features and adult support](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#reading-furenes); [Segal-Drori et al. · Adult-instructed e-book reading](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#reading-segal); [Munzer et al. · Parent–toddler reading interactions](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#reading-munzer).
   - Review flag: Thin for the size of the adult-support contribution across ages and tools; comparisons are not consistently matched.

5. **[Should students take notes by hand?](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#q5-handwritten-notes)**
   - Research label: mixed.
   - Existing evidence: [Mueller & Oppenheimer · Original longhand finding](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#notes-original); [Morehead et al. · Note-taking replication](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#notes-replication); [Flanigan et al. · Note-taking synthesis](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#notes-synthesis); [Van der Weel & Van der Meer · EEG, not achievement](https://bgraham14072.github.io/screen-use-academic-outcomes/#notes-eeg).
