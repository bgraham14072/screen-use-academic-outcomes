PRIMARY SCHOOL

Small gains, sensitive to study quality

Mobile literacy/numeracy interventions averaged d = 0.24. Removing five high-risk studies reduced the estimate to d = 0.14, with a confidence interval crossing zero. Dorris et al. ↗

PISA CONTEXT

A mixed pattern: limited use, heavier use and distraction

Up to one hour of school learning use was associated with +14 mathematics points; leisure use also had a positive adjusted association (+10), each versus none. Learning use of 5–7 hours versus 3–5 hours was associated with −10 points. These are different observational comparisons, not a recommended dose or causal effects.
