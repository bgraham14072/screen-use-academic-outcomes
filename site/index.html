Small gains, sensitive to study quality

Mobile literacy/numeracy interventions averaged d = 0.24. Removing five high-risk studies reduced the estimate to d = 0.14, with a confidence interval crossing zero. Dorris et al. ↗

PISA CONTEXT

Purpose is informative, not causal proof

Up to one hour of school learning use was associated with +14 math points; leisure use also had a positive adjusted association (+10). These contrasts do not establish a causal benefit or an optimal time limit. OECD ↗
