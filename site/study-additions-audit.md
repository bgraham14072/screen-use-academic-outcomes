## Findings preserved, including mixed and null results

- **Allcott:** Phone use fell substantially, while discipline initially increased and the effect faded; wellbeing initially declined suggestively and later recovered, with possible positive later effects ([original paper, abstract and pp. 2–4](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w35132/w35132.pdf)). Average academic effects were near zero, with modest positive high-school estimates, especially in mathematics, and small negative middle-school estimates; attendance, attention and perceived online bullying did not clearly improve ([original paper, abstract and pp. 3–4](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w35132/w35132.pdf)). Personal-phone restrictions are kept distinct from instructional-device policies, and causal identification assumptions and the cohort/time-pattern limitation are explicit ([original paper, pp. 1–4](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w35132/w35132.pdf)).
- **Bettinger:** The final journal article, not just the working paper or publisher abstract, was reviewed ([published article](http://rfairlie.sites.luskin.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2023/08/jpam-2023-marginal-returns-to-cal.pdf)). Mathematics improved relative to control at both assigned doses, but the higher dose added no significant mathematics gain; language findings were mixed and the higher-dose result was not a significant negative effect relative to control ([Table 4 and discussion, pp. 561–563](http://rfairlie.sites.luskin.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2023/08/jpam-2023-marginal-returns-to-cal.pdf)).
- **Ran:** Collaboration/communication, problem-solving, conceptual development and adaptive practice had larger estimates than materials delivery or monitoring alone; monitoring/assessment had an uncertain estimate ([Table 3, p. 266](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/epdf/10.1111/jcal.12611)). The card reports publication source, design, duration and testing-instrument moderators, nonsignificant post-hoc contrasts for publication year, sample size and grade, and sparse subgroup limitations, without treating moderator differences as randomized comparisons ([Tables 4–10 and discussion, pp. 267–271](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/epdf/10.1111/jcal.12611)).
- **Létourneau:** Generally favorable findings against traditional teaching are balanced with weaker, mixed and null findings against non-intelligent tutoring, heterogeneous designs and short interventions ([original review, pp. 3–9](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41539-025-00320-7.pdf)). The card explicitly separates general school-age tutoring from early-literacy AI for young children; formal risk-of-bias grading is NR, and the review reports no registration or prepared protocol ([original review, pp. 10–13](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41539-025-00320-7.pdf)).
- **Fernández-Batanero:** Inclusion, access, autonomy and participation findings are balanced with training, cost and access barriers; no pooled causal achievement effect is claimed ([original review, pp. 1919–1923](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s11423-022-10127-7.pdf)). The link from the disability exception does not turn the review into an evaluation of the bill or a legal eligibility determination ([S10685, operative §37(3), p. 7](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S10685)).
- **Vuorre:** Mixed, mostly small/null population-level associations are shown alongside existing harm-related evidence, not as a rebuttal; aggregated internet-adoption data cannot establish effects on individual children or classroom use ([original article, pp. 919–931](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid:f89a1517-7585-4404-9da3-43f96ff846b6/files/r76537263k)).
- **Odgers:** Small positive, negative and null associations, correlational limitations and differential vulnerability are all preserved; the review is not presented as proof of harmlessness or as classroom evidence ([original review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8221420/)).

## Withheld or not verified

These items are not used as dashboard evidence or entered as numerical results. No unresolved value has been silently repaired, converted or averaged.

- **Allcott intervals:** Compatible numerical confidence-interval endpoints were not verified; the tabulated standard errors and graphical intervals were not converted into numerical CIs ([Tables II–III, pp. 34–35](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w35132/w35132.pdf)).
- **Bettinger intervals and minutes:** The main achievement table reports standard errors rather than numerical CI endpoints, so no register estimate is added ([Table 4, p. 561](http://rfairlie.sites.luskin.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2023/08/jpam-2023-marginal-returns-to-cal.pdf)). Approximate time descriptions are omitted in favor of explicitly assigned item counts; they are not converted into a precise school-day screen-time threshold ([pp. 554–556 and Table 4 notes](http://rfairlie.sites.luskin.ucla.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/32/2023/08/jpam-2023-marginal-returns-to-cal.pdf)).
- **Ran reporting inconsistencies:** Some moderator-table study totals differ from the overall review count, and publication-bias reporting includes internally discordant statistics; these totals/statistics are withheld, with no clean-bias verdict assigned ([pp. 265–269, Tables 2, 3 and 7](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/epdf/10.1111/jcal.12611)). The overall effect, CI, review counts and heterogeneity used here are explicitly printed in the original article, rather than reconstructed from those tables ([pp. 258, 263, 266 and 269](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/epdf/10.1111/jcal.12611)).
- **Létourneau inconsistencies:** The stated country total conflicts with listed settings, the non-intelligent comparator denominator differs across passages, and a study-level effect/CI pairing uses incompatible-looking values; these are all omitted ([pp. 2–8](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41539-025-00320-7.pdf)). A pooled effect, pooled CI and formal risk-of-bias grade are NR, not supplied by this dashboard ([review methods and results](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41539-025-00320-7.pdf)).
- **Fernández-Batanero inconsistencies:** Search-flow totals conflict, appendix enumeration is incomplete, and at least one included-study classification appears inconsistent with the eligibility criteria; those figures and classifications are not reproduced as verified findings ([pp. 1914–1916 and 1924–1929](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s11423-022-10127-7.pdf)). The final included-article count is explicitly labeled as the authors’ reported count, not an independently reconciled census; derived percentages and aggregate participant totals are not supplied ([p. 1911 and appendix](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s11423-022-10127-7.pdf)).
- **Vuorre inconsistencies:** The introduction and mental-health methods disagree on country coverage, so that country total is omitted; no elapsed-year count is calculated from the printed year ranges ([pp. 918 and 925](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid:f89a1517-7585-4404-9da3-43f96ff846b6/files/r76537263k)). Bayesian regression estimates are not converted to academic intervention effect sizes ([methods, pp. 919 and 925–927](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid:f89a1517-7585-4404-9da3-43f96ff846b6/files/r76537263k)).
- **Odgers scope:** A unique aggregate participant count, common grade range and new pooled effect/CI are NR; overlapping review and primary-study samples are not added ([original review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8221420/)).

## Preservation and interpretation

The existing Furenes, Goodyear and Orben references were not added again. The original register file and earlier estimates remain unchanged, and the new numerical estimate stays in the existing g/intervention comparison group without any cross-scale conversion or combined ranking.

The sixteen question titles, five group headings, eight primary navigation groups and answer-field structure are preserved. Only the requested answer bodies and citations, the third pulling-back comparison row, and the disability-exception crosswalk are updated; the print brief is unchanged.
